Why Is My Content Not Being Cached?

Customer question

My website's response headers include Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0. Why isn't Cloudflare caching my content?

Customer-facing response

Hello,

Thank you for sharing your response headers. The Cache-Control header you provided is a strong clue as to why the content may not be cached.

In particular, no-store tells caches not to store the response, while no-cache means a stored response must be revalidated before it is reused. The other directives also indicate that the response should not be treated as fresh for normal reuse.

We should first confirm which response is being returned and whether the content is eligible for caching. HTML content is not necessarily cached by default, and the origin server's Cache-Control headers can affect Cloudflare's caching behaviour.

If the content is public and safe to cache, you can review the origin's cache headers and configure suitable Cloudflare Cache Rules or an appropriate Edge TTL. After making changes, test the response headers again and check the CF-Cache-Status header to understand whether the response was served from cache.

Please avoid caching private, personalised, or sensitive responses unless the configuration explicitly protects that content from being shared between users.

Kind regards,
Cloudflare Support

Troubleshooting rationale

The Cache-Control directives shown in the question indicate that the origin is instructing caches not to store or normally reuse the response. We need to verify the complete response and cache configuration before recommending changes.

Tools used and why

  • curl -I: Inspect HTTP response headers.
  • CF-Cache-Status: Check the reported cache outcome.
  • Cloudflare Cache Rules: Review cache eligibility and edge TTL settings.
